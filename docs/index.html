Political theory, Norway in the world, and the philosophy of mind.

I study political theory and philosophy at Leiden University (MSc Political Science; MPhil Philosophy of Law, Governance and Politics). Before that I worked at Norway's Permanent Mission to the UN in New York and with UNDP in Nepal. I also co-run Elida Studio, a Scandinavian creative studio.
