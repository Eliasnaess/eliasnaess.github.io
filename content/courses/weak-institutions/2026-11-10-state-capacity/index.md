**Readings**

- Jeffrey Herbst (1990), "War and the State in Africa", *International Security* 14(4)
- Wade M. Cole (2015), "Mind the Gap: State Capacity and the Implementation of Human Rights Treaties", *International Organization* 69(2)
- James C. Scott (1998), *Seeing Like a State*, ch. 1 "Nature and Space"

**Where to start:** both episodes take the readings in order, from Herbst's bellicist argument and Cole's three dimensions of capacity to Scott's scientific forestry, then put them in conversation and end with three points to raise in class. Pick the length that fits your commute. The infographics give the concepts, the three authors side by side, and each argument at a glance.
