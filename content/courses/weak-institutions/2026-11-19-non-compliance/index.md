summary: Why do people obey the state, and what does it mean when they don't? Levi and Sacks tie willing compliance to effective and fair government, Tsai shows Chinese villagers breaking rules to tell officials a policy is wrong, and Velasco-Guachalla and colleagues show Bolivia's COVID-19 rules splitting along lines of contested legitimacy.

**Readings**

- Margaret Levi and Audrey Sacks (2009), "Legitimating beliefs: Sources and indicators", *Regulation & Governance* 3
- Lily L. Tsai (2015), "Constructive Noncompliance", *Comparative Politics* 47(3)
- V. Ximena Velasco-Guachalla, Calla Hummel, Jami Nelson-Nuñez and Carew Boulding (2021), "Legitimacy and Policy during Crises: Subnational COVID-19 Responses in Bolivia", *Perspectives on Politics* 20(2)
