**Readings**

- Volha Charnysh (2022), "Explaining Out-Group Bias in Weak States: Religion and Legibility in the 1891/1892 Russian Famine", *World Politics* 74(2)
- Lily L. Tsai, Benjamin S. Morse and Robert A. Blair (2020), "Building Credibility and Cooperation in Low-Trust Settings: Persuasion and Source Accountability in Liberia During the 2014–2015 Ebola Crisis", *Comparative Political Studies* 53(10–11)
- Shelby Grossman, Jonathan Phillips and Leah R. Rosenzweig (2017), "Opportunistic Accountability: State–Society Bargaining Over Shared Interests", *Comparative Political Studies*
- Matthew Amengual and Eduardo Dargent (2020), "The Social Determinants of Enforcement", in Brinks, Levitsky and Murillo (eds.), *The Politics of Institutional Weakness in Latin America*, ch. 7

**Where to start:** both episodes take the readings in order, from Charnysh's legibility argument and the Ebola canvassers to vaccine bargaining in northern Nigeria and Amengual and Dargent's enforcement typology, then put them in conversation and end with three points to raise in class.
