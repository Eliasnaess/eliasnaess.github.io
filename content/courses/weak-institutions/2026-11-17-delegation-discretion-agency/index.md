**Readings**

- Tucker Van Aken and Orion A. Lewis (2015), "The Political Economy of Noncompliance in China: the case of industrial energy policy", *Journal of Contemporary China* 24(95)
- Mai Hassan (2015), "Continuity despite change: Kenya's new constitution and executive power", *Democratization* 22(4)
- Gabriela Lotta, Fernando Nieto-Morales and Rik Peeters (2024), "Street-level bureaucracy in weak state institutions: an introduction", ch. 1 of Peeters, Lotta and Nieto-Morales (eds.), *Street-Level Bureaucracy in Weak State Institutions* (Bristol University Press)
- Abdul-Rahim Mohammed (2024), "'You can tell they are just villagers when you look at them': a phenomenological study of street-level bureaucrats' differential treatment of clients in a Ghanaian rural hospital", ch. 10 of the same volume
- David Ehrhardt (2017), "Indigeneship, bureaucratic discretion, and institutional change in Northern Nigeria", *African Affairs* 116(464)

**Where to start:** both episodes take the readings from the centre outwards: provinces, then the executive bureaucracy, then the frontline, then ambiguity, and ends by asking whether this kind of weakness is chosen or suffered. Infographics are on their way.
