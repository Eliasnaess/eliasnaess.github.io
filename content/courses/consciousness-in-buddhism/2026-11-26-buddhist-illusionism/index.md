**Readings:**
- Xuanzang, *Demonstration of Consciousness Only*, "The Three Natures", in *Three Texts on Consciousness Only* (trans. Cook, 1999), pp. 281–296.
- Garfield, Jay L. (2022). "Cognitive Illusion and Immediate Experience: Perspectives from Buddhist Philosophy." In *Cross-Cultural Approaches to Consciousness*, ed. Shani and Beiweis, pp. 245–265.
- Huebner, Bryce, Eyal Aviv and Sonam Kachru (2022). "The Magic of Consciousness: Sculpting an Alternative Illusionism." In *Cross-Cultural Approaches to Consciousness*, pp. 223–244.

**Where to start:** the brief sets the three readings side by side: Xuanzang's three natures as the Yogācāra account of what the illusion is, Garfield on why even self-knowledge is mediated, and Huebner, Aviv and Kachru's magic show as an alternative to Dennett and Frankish. Both episodes run about 25 minutes; the first infographic compares all four positions in one table.

**Notes on the infographics:** infographic 1 has typos ("contest-dependent" for context-dependent, "kormic", "micropresents", "causel", "deplay"). It also gives Huebner, Aviv and Kachru's mechanism as "root awareness (ālaya-vijñāna)"; in the chapter the mechanism is attention sculpted by stances and practice, and the "root strategy" is the magician's trick, not the store consciousness.
